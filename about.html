Signaturesi

One ecosystem. Two core products. One long-term research direction.

Signaturesi is focused on two immediate product experiences: NEO for personal intelligence and Bean for communication.

Bean ID connects the ecosystem through one account, while Green AI research explores how capable language systems may become smaller, more efficient and more practical on personal devices.

Our mission

Make useful intelligence more personal, direct and efficient.

Today’s most capable AI systems often depend on large remote infrastructure. Signaturesi is exploring a future where more intelligence can live closer to the user, understand personal context and operate with less computational waste.

The objective is not to add AI everywhere. The objective is to build a smaller number of focused products that are genuinely useful.

Mission statement
Build technology that feels intelligent without becoming distant, complicated or unnecessarily heavy.

Green AI research

Intelligence should not always require massive infrastructure.

Signaturesi research is exploring how language systems could become smaller, modular and more efficient while retaining useful reasoning and language understanding.

The long-term direction is mobile-first intelligence: systems capable of operating closer to the user with lower latency, greater privacy potential and reduced dependency on remote compute.

Product principles

What Signaturesi is trying to protect.

01

Focus before expansion

Build NEO and Bean properly before expanding into unrelated products.

02

Simplicity before decoration

Interfaces should remain clear, calm and useful rather than visually overloaded.

03

Honest capability claims

Product descriptions should reflect what has actually been implemented and tested.

04

User control

People should understand and control their identity, memory, communication and product settings.

05

Efficient intelligence

Better systems should use computation deliberately, not wastefully.

06

Security by architecture

Trust must come from access control, testing and engineering—not marketing language.
