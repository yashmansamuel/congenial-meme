Security

Account protection and reporting security problems.

Protect your account

Use a unique password, keep recovery details current and sign out of shared devices. Do not share passwords, session tokens or recovery codes. Review available account-security settings and report unexpected access.

Understand the protections

Use the official HTTPS addresses for Signaturesi products. HTTPS protects the connection to a service; it does not establish end-to-end encryption of stored messages, files or calls. This page does not claim a security certification or an independent audit.

Report a security issue

Report the issue privately to signaturesi@bean on Bean. Include the affected product, a description, safe reproduction steps and the possible impact. Do not include passwords, access tokens or other users’ private information.

Keep testing safe

Do not access other users’ data, disrupt the service or run destructive tests. Stop if you encounter private data and describe the issue without copying that data. A report does not grant permission to test systems beyond your own authorized access.
