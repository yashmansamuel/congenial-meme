01Overview

Your information should have a clear purpose.

Signaturesi develops connected products for artificial intelligence and communication, including NEO, Bean and Bean ID. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, shared and deleted when you use Signaturesi websites, applications and services.

In this policy, “Signaturesi,” “we,” “our” and “us” refer to the operator of the Signaturesi services. The final legal entity responsible for the services must be inserted before public release.

Required before launch Replace this paragraph with the complete legal name of the entity operating Signaturesi.

02Scope

Where this policy applies.

This policy applies to information processed through:

  • The Signaturesi website and related webpages.
  • NEO, including AI conversations, workspace features, personal memory and related account functions.
  • Bean, including messaging, presence, calling and communication features.
  • Bean ID, including account creation, authentication and unified identity features.
  • Support, security, research participation and product feedback channels operated by Signaturesi.

This policy does not automatically apply to third-party websites or services linked from Signaturesi. Those services may operate under their own privacy policies.

Signaturesi may collect information you provide, information created through your use of the services, and limited technical information generated by your browser or device.

05NEO data

AI interactions and personal memory.

When you use NEO, information may be processed to understand your request, generate a response, maintain conversation context and provide workspace functions.

NEO AI workspace Data categories
Prompts and responses

Text, instructions and generated content within your NEO conversations.

Uploaded content

Images, files or documents you deliberately add for analysis or project work.

Conversation context

Temporary context used to keep the current conversation coherent.

Personal memory

Preferences or project information saved through an enabled memory feature.

Personal memory controls

A production version of NEO should provide clear controls to view, correct, disable and delete durable personal memory. Signaturesi should not describe these controls as available until they are fully implemented.

Model improvement

Signaturesi should not use private NEO conversations to train or improve general AI models unless the user is clearly informed and has provided an appropriate choice or consent. The final policy must reflect the actual production practice.

Recommended production position

Private NEO conversations should not be used for general model training by default.

06Bean communication data

Messaging and communication information.

When you use Bean, Signaturesi may process information required to deliver messages, maintain conversations, show availability and support voice or video calls.

Bean Communication Data categories
Message content

Text, voice messages, images, videos, files and reactions shared through Bean.

Communication metadata

Sender, recipient, timestamps, delivery status and conversation identifiers.

Presence information

Online status, last active state, typing status or availability signals.

Call information

Participants, start time, end time, call status and limited connection diagnostics.

Encryption claims

Do not claim that Bean uses end-to-end encryption until the complete encryption protocol has been implemented, independently reviewed and verified across messages, media backups and calls.

Current wording Bean is being designed with privacy and controlled access as core product requirements.

07Automatically collected information

Technical information used to operate the service.

When you access Signaturesi, certain technical information may be generated automatically. Depending on the final implementation, this may include:

  • Browser type, operating system and application version.
  • Device category, screen size and language settings.
  • Approximate location inferred from an internet protocol address.
  • Login time, session activity and authentication events.
  • Feature usage, page navigation and performance measurements.
  • Crash reports, diagnostic logs and security events.

Signaturesi should minimize this information and avoid collecting precise location, contact lists, microphone, camera or device files unless a feature requires access and the user provides permission.

Signaturesi may use information for the following purposes:

02 Personalize your experience

03 Maintain security

Detect unauthorized access, fraud, abuse, automated attacks and policy violations.

Investigate errors, measure performance and improve the stability of Signaturesi products.

05 Communicate with users

06 Meet legal obligations

Respond to valid legal requests and maintain records where required.

10Information sharing

Signaturesi should not sell personal information.

Signaturesi does not intend to sell personal information to advertisers or data brokers. Information may be disclosed only in limited circumstances, including:

  • To service providers supporting Signaturesi operations.
  • When you direct Signaturesi to share information or connect an external service.
  • To protect users, Signaturesi or the public from fraud, abuse or security threats.
  • To comply with a valid legal requirement.
  • As part of a merger, acquisition, financing or transfer of business assets, subject to appropriate protections and notice where required.

Aggregated or de-identified information may be used for analytics and research where it cannot reasonably identify an individual.

11Data retention

Keep information only as long as it remains necessary.

Retention periods depend on the type of information, the feature being used, security needs and applicable legal requirements.

Account data While the account remains active

Some records may be retained briefly after deletion for recovery, fraud prevention or legal requirements.

NEO conversations Based on user settings

Conversation deletion and history controls should be implemented before final publication.

Bean messages Based on delivery and storage design

The final policy must match the actual message storage and backup architecture.

Security logs Limited operational period

May be retained to investigate unauthorized access, abuse or system incidents.

Deleting information from an active interface may not immediately remove encrypted backups or temporary recovery copies. Those copies should expire through a defined backup lifecycle.

12Security

Security is a continuous process, not an absolute guarantee.

Signaturesi intends to use technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the services. These may include:

Access controls

Restrict internal and system access to authorized purposes.

Account isolation

Prevent one user from accessing another user’s information.

Secure transmission

Protect data while it moves between supported systems.

Monitoring

Detect suspicious activity, failures and security incidents.

No online service can guarantee complete security. Users should protect their login credentials, use trusted devices and report suspected unauthorized access promptly.
