Privacy

How information is handled across Signaturesi, NEYO, Bean, UAsset and Bean ID.

Information we process

Account and profile details identify your Bean ID. Depending on the features you use, service data can include AI prompts and replies, uploaded files, saved preferences, messages, call information and payment status. Technical records can include device information, errors and login activity.

Why it is processed

Information is used to provide the features you request, maintain accounts, deliver communications, process subscriptions, investigate faults and prevent abuse. Permissions such as microphone, camera and file access relate to the features you choose to use.

Service providers and sharing

Hosting, database, AI and payment providers can process information needed for their services. AI requests can send your prompt, relevant conversation context and selected files to an AI provider. Information you share with another person is also available to that recipient. Provider practices and processing locations depend on the product and integration.

Storage and deletion

Account data, saved content and operational records can have different retention periods. Deleting content from an interface does not necessarily remove backup copies immediately. Use available account controls or contact Signaturesi to ask about access, correction, export or deletion. Account ownership may need to be verified before a request is processed.

Cookies and browser storage

Cookies and browser storage can keep you signed in and remember product settings. Third-party services, including payment or embedded services, may use their own storage. You can inspect, block or delete site data through your browser settings; doing so can sign you out or reset preferences. Optional tracking and consent requirements depend on the technologies enabled in each product.

Questions and changes

Message signaturesi@bean on Bean for privacy questions. This policy will be updated when data practices change. Your privacy rights depend on applicable law.
